Why quiet interfaces are easier to love

Author Name · 6 min read

Most apps shout. They badge, bounce, vibrate and blink until you forget what you came for. The ones we keep coming back to do something rarer: they stay out of the way.

That does not mean they are plain. A quiet interface is built with restraint, and restraint takes more decisions than decoration ever does.

Motion with a job

Good motion explains. It shows where a thing came from and where it went. When an element slides in from the edge, it tells you more lives out there.

If you notice the animation, it is probably doing too much.

Three rules

We use three rules when we add movement to a page:

  • Keep it short. Under a second, almost always.
  • Move things a small distance, not across the screen.
  • Respect people who ask for less motion.

Try it

Turn on “reduce motion” in your system settings and reload. Everything still appears, it just does not move.

Where to go next

Start with one element. Pick the most important sentence on the page, give it a soft entrance and leave everything else alone. You will be surprised how far one considered detail goes.
