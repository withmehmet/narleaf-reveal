FAQ

Good questions.

Still unsure? Write to us and a person will answer.

Is there a free plan?

Yes. Starter is free for up to three members and never expires.

Can I import my existing tasks?

You can import from CSV in two minutes. Boards, owners and due dates are kept.

How does the yearly discount work?

Pay for twelve months upfront and save 20%. You can switch back to monthly at renewal.

Where is my data stored?

In encrypted storage, backed up daily. Business plans can choose a region.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Cancel from settings and keep read-only access until the end of the period.
