Process

Simple steps, no surprises.

  1. Listen

    A short call to understand goals, limits and the people you build for.

  2. Explore

    Rough ideas first. We pick a direction together before any polish.

  3. Refine

    Weekly reviews, clear feedback rounds and files that stay organised.

  4. Hand over

    Ready-to-build files, notes for developers and support after launch.
